## Full Description

**Scan anything. Instantly.**

Scanner Pro is the fastest, cleanest QR code and barcode scanner on Android. Point your camera, get the result — no ads interrupting your flow, no wasted seconds.

---

### Why Scanner Pro?

Most scanner apps are bloated, slow, or covered in pop-ups. Scanner Pro does one thing and does it perfectly: scans anything your camera can see and puts the result in your hands immediately.

**⚡ Lightning-Fast Scanning**
Results appear the moment your camera focuses. No button to press, no waiting — just point and scan. Works with QR codes, barcodes, URLs, Wi-Fi codes, contact cards, and more.

**🎨 Powerful QR Code Generator**
Create custom QR codes for any content — URLs, text, email, phone numbers, Wi-Fi credentials, and more. Customize colors, download as PNG or high-quality PDF, and share anywhere.

**📋 Full Scan History**
Every scan is saved automatically. Search, revisit, copy, or share any past result without scanning again. Your history stays private — stored only on your device.

**⭐ Favorites**
Star the codes you use most — Wi-Fi passwords, business cards, links — and access them with one tap. No more re-scanning the same codes.

**🔒 100% Private**
Scanner Pro works entirely offline for scanning and generating. No account needed. No data sent to any server. What you scan stays on your phone.

### What Scanner Pro reads:
✓ QR Codes (all types)
✓ Barcodes (EAN-13, EAN-8, UPC-A, UPC-E)
✓ Data Matrix & Aztec codes
✓ URLs, email, phone, SMS
✓ Wi-Fi credentials (auto-connect)
✓ vCard contact codes
✓ Plain text
